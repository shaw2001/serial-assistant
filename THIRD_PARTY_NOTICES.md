## OPPO Sans

界面使用 OPPO 官方提供的原始 `OPPOSans OS2.0 GB2312 Design Regular`，不裁剪、不转换、不改名字。

来源：https://code.oppo.com/content/dam/oppo/common/fonts/font2/new-font/OPPOSansOS2-5000-Regular.woff2

SHA-256：`54c935e46cce719f92db964976cbeb5c1da081b42114ef5e8866d64f2e5a19bb`

字体版权属于 OPPO 及其权利人，并不受本项目代码许可覆盖。发布版本包含原始字体用于界面显示，构建时由官方来源获取；如需修改字体或单独再分发，应核对权利人的授权条件。

- golang.org/x/sys and golang.org/x/text: BSD-3-Clause, https://cs.opensource.google/go/x/sys and https://cs.opensource.google/go/x/text .
- jchv/go-webview2 and jchv/go-winloader: MIT, https://github.com/jchv/go-webview2 and https://github.com/jchv/go-winloader . WebView2Loader.dll is the Microsoft SDK redistributable embedded by go-webview2; https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Web.WebView2 .

The v0.1.1 EXE does not bundle Electron, Node.js or the Chromium/WebView2 runtime. Microsoft WebView2 Runtime is a separately serviced system dependency. UI assets and the pinned original OPPO font are embedded in the EXE.
